Every transaction is a 100-millisecond decision.

02  The problem

Static rules force an impossible trade-off.

Legacy fraud engines are frozen if-then rules. Tighten them and you decline good customers; loosen them and fraud slips through. Either way, a human analyst reviews the aftermath hours later — long after the money is gone.

Fraud slips through

Rules only catch patterns someone already wrote down. Novel attacks pass untouched.

False declines

Good customers blocked at checkout. The single biggest driver of churn and lost revenue.

Slow to act

Manual review comes hours later. Explanations are reconstructed after the fact.

No memory

Every transaction judged cold. The system never learns that this customer is legitimate.

03  The Aegis solution

A team of AI agents that investigates every risky payment.

Aegis replaces the static rulebook with an autonomous investigation. Low-risk payments clear in milliseconds; anything suspicious is handed to a team of specialist Gemini 3.5 agents that gather evidence, weigh it, and return a decision with its reasons — then verify the customer instead of simply declining them.

Autonomous

Investigates, not just scores

Agents pull evidence, debate, and reach a verdict — no analyst in the loop.

Adaptive

Verify, don't decline

Borderline? Step up with an OTP instead of losing a good customer.

Explainable

Reason codes + evidence

Every decision ships with confidence, reason codes and an evidence summary.

Remembers

Customer memory

Firestore memory of confirmed-legit behavior and tier means fewer repeat challenges.

05  Tyson user flow  / step-up in action

The same payment. Two very different outcomes.

T
Tyson · large auto-pay charge
A high-value payment fires. Aegis checks card status, then the step-up policy gate before it ever reaches the agents.
◆ auto-pay OFF · no OTP verified
✕ Card blocked

Unverified high-value charge with auto-pay disabled — Aegis refuses to let it through and protects the customer. The card is held pending verification, with a reason code the analyst can see instantly.

◆ OTP verified / auto-pay ON
✓ Approved

The customer confirms with a Mobile or Email OTP — or auto-pay was intentionally enabled. Aegis verifies identity and approves the exact same payment. A good customer is kept, not declined.

The lesson: verify the customer, don't just decline the charge.

05  Behind the scenes  / how the agents work

An investigation team, not a single model call.

When the deep path fires, an Orchestrator convenes specialists on Gemini 3.5. Each has one job; a Critic keeps them honest. You watch every step stream live in the console.

Orchestrator
Convenes the team, routes evidence, and issues the final adaptive decision.
Card Status

Step-Up Control

Investigator

Network Analyst

Intel

Compliance

Critic
Challenges the verdict and stress-tests confidence before it ships.
